HOME FLASH DEMO

A virtual pet you raise out of a computer virus.

Malwarium is a handheld for a little ESP32 gadget — Tamagotchi, but you're a hacker and the pet is a malbeast you caught out on the 'net and tamed. Hatch it, feed it, defrag it, take it exploring, and find out whether it grows into a well-behaved daemon or something considerably worse.

This is the publish host. It holds the firmware a device downloads when it updates itself, and a flasher for the updates that have to travel down a cable.

GET IT ONTO A DEVICE

⚡ FLASH OVER USB →
Writes a whole working device from your browser — bootloader, partition table and firmware. Needs Chrome or Edge on a computer, and a USB-C cable. No toolchain, no command line. This is also how you rescue a device that won't boot.
((·)) OVER WI-FI, FROM THE DEVICE
Most updates need no cable at all. On the device: CFG → UPDATES → CHECK NOW. It joins your network for the length of the job, asks before it installs anything, and hands the radio back when it's done.

IT WON'T BOOTUSB

An over-the-air update writes into a spare app slot and nothing else, so it can never replace the bootloader or the partition table. When a release changes one of those, the cable is the only way in — which is the entire reason the flasher exists.
