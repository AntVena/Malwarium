HOME FLASH DEMO

Try it before you order a board.

Malwarium is a virtual pet you raise out of a computer virus. It runs on a little ESP32 handheld that you buy and flash yourself — and this page is that same firmware, running in a browser tab, with a pet somebody already raised. Have a poke around and see if it's your kind of thing before you go and order hardware for it.

WHAT THIS ISN'T

NOTHING HERE STICKS AROUND Your pet lives in this browser's local storage and nowhere else. Clear your site data and it's gone. Open a private window and it was never there. Sit down at another computer and you get a fresh one.

The save format also moves as the firmware gains things, and old formats get retired eventually. On a device that's fine — an update migrates your pet forward. A browser tab can't be checked on the same way, so sooner or later an update here will just hand you a new pet instead. Treat this as a test drive, not a pet.
THE SLOW BIT IS THE POINT On a device you start with an encrypted egg and about half an hour of incubation, and after that you're raising the thing over days. It gets hungry while you're not looking. What it grows into depends on how you treated it. None of that fits in a browser session, so the demo skips it and hands you a pet that's already six levels in with things to spend. Just know that the part you're not seeing is the part people keep the device around for.
NO RADIO IN A BROWSER CFG, SCAN, LINK and PEERS sit greyed out on the shelf. That's the Wi-Fi half: joining a network, sniffing the air for networks to catch and feed to your pet, linking up with someone else's Malwarium for a duel, pulling down its own updates. None of it exists in a browser tab, so those slots stay shut rather than opening onto a screen that could only ever say nothing found.

WHAT'S ACTUALLY REAL

The game. This is the device's firmware compiled to WebAssembly — same C++ engine, same 240×240 panel, same three buttons. Nothing was rebuilt for the web, so the fights, the arcade, the items and the art are exactly what runs on the hardware. If you like it here, you'll like it there.
⚡ PUT IT ON A REAL ONE →
Malwarium runs on one specific board, a Waveshare ESP32-S3-LCD-1.54. Get one, plug it in over USB, and the flasher writes the whole thing from your browser — no toolchain, no command line. That version keeps your pet.
