A+C together is the Exploit chord — it flips to the Hacker face, and opens the override picker in a fight. Keyboard: Z / X / C, or the arrow keys.

WHAT THIS IS

This is the device's own firmware — the same C++ engine, the same 240×240 panel, the same three buttons — compiled to WebAssembly and drawn onto a canvas. Nothing is re-implemented for the web, so what happens here is what happens on the hardware.

WHERE TO START

EXPLTake the pet out on the 'net. Fights, loot, and five areas deep.
GAMESThe arcade — four cabinets, including a snake and a mastermind board.
ITEMSYou start with food, buffs and a few Decryptograms.
A+C → VAULTCash a Decryptogram for a quote board.
MAINTDefrag the pet. It is a puzzle, not a chore.
THE DEMO SKIPS THE FIRST HALF-HOUR A real device starts with an encrypted egg and a ~30-minute incubation you spend earning down. This drops you onto a pet already six levels in, with things to spend, so there is something to look at straight away.
WHAT THE BROWSER CANNOT DO CFG, SCAN, LINK and PEERS are dimmed here. They are the radio and the device settings — joining Wi-Fi, sniffing the air for networks to tame, linking to another Malwarium to duel it, installing an update. A browser has no radio, so those slots stay on the shelf rather than opening onto a screen that could only ever say nothing found. They are the half of the device this demo cannot show.
